Official timetable

Published Eastern and Western Zone schedules republished from Bangladesh Railway Timetable No. 54 (effective 10 March 2025). This is a published timetable, not live running status: no location, delay, ETA, platform, seat availability or fare is shown.

Journey Board Official e-ticket portal Source index

Filters match the published origin and destination only. The source summary tables do not list intermediate stops, so a search for an intermediate pair returns nothing instead of a guessed route. Weekly off days and times are printed exactly as published; an arrival at or before the departure time is shown as next day.
